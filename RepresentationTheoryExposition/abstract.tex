\begin{center}
    \Large
    \textbf{Introduction}
    
    \vspace{0.4cm}
    \large

\end{center}
This exposition aims at exploring the basic foundations of representation theory of finite groups. The main outcome is to explore Maschke's theorem that provides a sufficient condition for when a group algebra is semisimple. We also explore Artin-Wedderburn theorem that characterizes the structure of the semisimple algebras irreducible and finite dimensional representations. The exposition assumes basic knowledge in group, ring and field theory and linear algebra
